Beyond the Curve

An intervention community built on one bold belief: an online community can change real lives and impact real outcomes.

The Mission

Community is the intervention.

Beyond the Curve focuses on the people most at risk of returning to the justice system: those 34 and younger serving shorter sentences, who are statistically far more likely to re-offend after release.

Through the Forward Living program, members take educational courses in prison, engage in volunteerism through their facility, and connect to an online mentorship and intervention community upon release. The goal is straightforward: keep people moving forward, because strong community support moves individuals forward and poor community support takes them backwards.
