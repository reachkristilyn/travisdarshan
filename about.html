About Me

I'm an author, researcher, and entrepreneur drawn to one question: how do people and systems break through their circumstances?

That question runs through everything I do. It's in my fiction, where characters wrestle with the circumstances they were handed. It's in my nonfiction, a practical system for growing through hardship rather than being defined by it. And it's at the heart of Beyond the Curve, the nonprofit I lead to support young people in the justice system.

On the technical side, I work on research projects in mathematics and artificial intelligence, including certification from MIT.

As an entrepreneur, I'm interested in building things that should exist but don't yet.
